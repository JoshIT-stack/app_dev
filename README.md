# My Favorite Series: Grimm

![Grimm](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSozo-GWPwotpDQRyEe8LkDvOCp8qDRbUmS_nBA47a7SA&s=10)

## About the Show
**Grimm** is a fantasy-crime drama about *Nick Burkhardt*, a homicide detective in Portland, Oregon. He discovers he is a **Grimm**, a descendant of a long line of hunters who can see monsters (called *Wesen*) hiding in human form.

## The Key and the Box
Early in the series, Nick's dying Aunt Marie gives him a strange key. It becomes the show's biggest mystery:

1. Nick learns the key is one of **seven** keys passed down from the ancestors of the Grimms.
2. He and his friends slowly collect them across the seasons.
3. The keys lead to a hidden box in Germany's Black Forest.
4. Nick's own blood is needed to break the seal on the lid.

## Main Characters
- Nick Burkhardt (the Grimm)
- Hank Griffin
  - Nick's partner and fellow detective
- Monroe
  - A reformed Wesen who becomes Nick's best friend
- Rosalee Calvert
